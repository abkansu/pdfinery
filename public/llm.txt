# PDFinery - Free Secure PDF Toolkit

> PDFinery is a comprehensive, privacy-focused PDF toolkit that runs entirely in your browser. Merge, split, convert, edit, sign, secure, and optimize PDF documents securely without uploading files to any server.

## Core Capabilities
- **Merge & Organize**: Combine multiple PDF files into a single document. Drag-and-drop to reorder pages or delete unwanted pages.
- **Convert PDF**: Convert PDF pages to high-quality JPG or PNG images. Extract text from PDF documents instantly. Turn JPG, PNG images, and text files into multi-page PDF documents.
- **Split & Extract**: Split PDFs by custom page ranges, extract specific pages, or split a document into single individual pages.
- **Edit PDF**: Add text, shapes, freehand drawings, and images directly onto PDF pages.
- **Sign & Secure**: Visually add your signature to documents. Lock, unlock, and restrict editing permissions for your PDFs.
- **Optimize PDF**: Compress PDFs to reduce file size, adjust image quality, and remove metadata.
- **Internationalization**: Full support for English and Turkish languages.
- **Privacy First**: 100% client-side processing; files never leave the user's device. No server uploads.
- **No Limits**: Free to use with no file size limits, no watermarks, and no accounts required.

## Technical Context
- **Security**: Uses client-side WASM and JS libraries (pdf-lib) for manipulation. No backend storage or cloud processing.
- **Performance**: Optimized for modern browsers with near zero-latency operations after load.
- **Compatibility**: Works on all major desktop and mobile browsers.
